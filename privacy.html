Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

About the Application

LSA Offer Monitor is a personal-use application that monitors authorized Gmail messages for interpreter assignment offers.

Information Accessed

The application uses Google Gmail API access to read email messages relevant to interpreter assignment notifications.

How Information Is Used

Email information is used to identify relevant assignment offers and facilitate their acceptance.

Data Storage

The application operates on the owner's personal computer. Google authorization credentials are stored locally to maintain authorized access.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell Gmail data or share email contents with advertisers. Relevant assignment acceptance links are accessed through the assignment provider's website to accept offers.

Data Retention and Deletion

The owner can stop the application and revoke its Google account access through Google Account security settings. Locally stored authorization credentials can also be deleted.

Security

The application uses Google's OAuth authorization system and runs on a personal computer controlled by its owner.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
